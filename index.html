CT Puzzle : Tangram

CT Puzzle : Tangram faithfully implements the interactive mathematical tangram puzzle designed by Cleve Moler (founder of MathWorks, creators of MATLAB).

Computational Thinking Core

  • Decomposition: Break complex shapes into 7 standard Euclidean polygons (2 large triangles, 1 medium triangle, 2 small triangles, 1 square, 1 parallelogram).
  • Algorithmic Transformations: Translate $\vec{v} + \Delta\vec{z}$, rotate through complex multiples $e^{i\theta}$ ($\theta = \pi/20 = 9^\circ$), and reflect the chiral parallelogram across its vertical axis.
  • Pattern Recognition & Convergence: Nearest-vertex Euclidean distance calculation $\min |z_k - w_j| < 0.5$, magnetically snapping pieces into exact geometric alignment.

Controls & Gestures

  • Move Piece: Click/touch near the center (centroid) of a piece and drag.
  • Rotate Piece: Click/touch near an outer vertex and drag, or use the mouse scroll wheel over any piece. Increments are quantized to $9^\circ$ ($\pi/20$).
  • Flip Parallelogram: Select Piece #7 and press F or click Flip (#7).
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: R Rotate +9°, Shift + R Rotate -9°, F Flip #7, Esc Deselect.
  • Magnetic Snapping: Release a piece near an adjacent edge or corner to snap it with audio-visual feedback.
